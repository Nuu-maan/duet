# Code of conduct

Duet is a small project made for people who care about each other. Please bring the same attitude here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Discuss the idea, not the person. Disagree with reasons.
- Assume good intent, and say so plainly when something bothers you.
- Keep questions, answers and examples friendly for everyone. Duet is used by couples and by friends.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information
- Anything you would not say to a colleague face to face

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block repeat
offenders. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.

This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
